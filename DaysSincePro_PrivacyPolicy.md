# Privacy Policy — DaysSincePro (Android)

DaysSincePro by MerWare does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.  
The app runs entirely on your device and does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or any form of tracking.  
No data is sent to MerWare or any third parties.

## Local‑Only Operation

All app functionality, including date tracking and calculations, occurs entirely on your device.  
The app does not require network access and does not communicate with external servers.

## Local Backup and Export

DaysSincePro provides optional backup and restore features using a local SQLite database file, as well as import and export via CSV.  
These files are created and stored locally on the user’s device.  
The app does not automatically upload, sync, or transmit backup files.  
All backup, restore, import, and export actions are user‑initiated and remain fully local.

## Permissions

DaysSincePro requests only the minimal permissions required for basic functionality and for user‑initiated backup/export operations.  
The app does not access contacts, location, photos, media, or any personal data.
